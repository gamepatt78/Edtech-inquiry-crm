# Inquiry message examples

Use these as inspiration for the **Tell us a little more** field. Include what you want to know and any details that will help the team respond. Avoid sharing sensitive information.

## Scholarship

> I’m a student interested in scholarship opportunities for next year. Could you tell me what scholarships are available, the eligibility requirements, and when applications close?

## Concert

> I’m a parent looking for information about the upcoming student concert. Could you share the date, venue, ticket details, and whether students need to register?

## Admission

> I’m interested in the admission process for next term. Could you share the requirements, important dates, and how to arrange a visit?

## General query

> I’m a school representative exploring your programs for our students. Could someone contact me to discuss the options and how we can get started?
