# Inquiry examples

These 10 complete fictional submissions show how to fill in every field in the public inquiry form. The email addresses use the reserved `example.com` domain, and the phone numbers use fictional 555 numbers. Do not use real people's personal information in test submissions.

## 1. Scholarship eligibility

- **User type:** Student
- **Category:** Scholarship
- **Name:** Alex Morgan
- **Email:** alex.morgan@example.com
- **Phone:** +1 202-555-0101
- **Message:** I’m interested in scholarships for next academic year. Could you share the eligibility requirements, application deadlines, and documents I should prepare?
